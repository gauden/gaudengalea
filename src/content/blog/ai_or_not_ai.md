“Fear is not a strategy.”

That line became the centre of a short, hopefully light-hearted, video I made for a conference session on artificial intelligence and public health. Rather than give a conventional talk, I staged a conversation between two simulated AI agents: **Dystostrich**, who sees the risks of AI arriving faster than governance, and **Owltopia**, who sees the practical value of careful, responsible adoption.

Dystostrich worries about energy use, misinformation, cyber-risk, degraded evidence, and platform power. Owltopia argues that AI can help public health with surveillance, synthesis, early warning, triage, and better use of scarce human time.

I argue that public health has to engage with both sides seriously. I emphasise three practical points on engagement that are crucial for public health: governance, R & D / evaluation, personal productivity. Comment in the video discussion to give your take.

Watch the video: [AI or Not AI? Conversations with My Agents](https://youtu.be/r0S_aZ-7LZY)
