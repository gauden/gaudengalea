## TL;DR

My review of Silo, the Apple TV series, reveals me to be addicted despite its obvious shortcomings. Obsessed enough to create [a systems map](https://gauden.github.io/silo-systems-map/) of the key elements, loops, pathways, and contradictions for your exploration and enjoyment.

## A Reluctant Convert

I came late and reluctantly to [Silo](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/silo/umc.cmc.3yksgc857px0k0rqe5zd4jice), the Apple TV series that has just ended its third season and teased us already with a [trailer to the fourth](https://youtu.be/8Yq7dvVINpY?si=1DX33ekV1_dylYZ4). I did not find the premise of "thousands living in a silo underground" in a distant and toxic future very convincing. But I must admit that I found myself unable to turn away, and increasingly addicted with each new season and now join the fanbase in highly anticipating the release of the concluding season in summer of 2027.

I will not dwell on the many strong points in the series, just to name a few. The characters, led by Juliette Nichols (Rebecca Ferguson) are plucky, curious, passionate and even the villains often turn out to be heroes in most cases, or to have a heroic moment. The plot zooms out from the everyday micro-concerns of the population of one silo (who will win the reproduction lottery this year?), to the discovery of other siloed populations (we are not alone), to a growing macro-level understanding of why they are all trapped/sheltered in these holes for centuries. Hugh Howley's trilogy (which I have not yet read) has provided Graham Yost with material for a rich and tantalising epic, and a plot that spans four centuries full of twists, dark visions, and people you can't help caring about.

## Breaking "Silo"

Despite all this, there are so many ways the internal logic of the habitat breaks down, that I couldn't help but obsess over little details. And the details mounted till I couldn't resist first sketching then using the newly released Astra model by OpenAI to create a systems map that summarises all the key elements, connections, and contradictions in the Silo universe. Have a look at it here on Github Pages: https://gauden.github.io/silo-systems-map/
